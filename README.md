# Wordbook

A personal vocabulary learning - flashcard app for discovering, collecting, and learning English words through AI-powered tools.

**Live Demo:** [wordbook-nine.vercel.app](https://wordbook-nine.vercel.app/)

## Features

- **AI Word Lookup** — generate word meanings, parts of speech, example sentences, translations, and notes.
- **Vocabulary Collection** — save and manage your personal word list.
- **Flashcards** — review vocabulary through individual word cards.
- **Talk with AI** — practice English and explore vocabulary through voice conversations powered by Vapi.
- **User Authentication** — sign in and access your personal vocabulary collection.
- **Cloud Database** — store vocabulary entries in Supabase.
- **Local Storage** — keep a local copy of your vocabulary in the browser.

## Tech Stack

- [Next.js](https://nextjs.org/)
- [React](https://react.dev/)
- [TypeScript](https://www.typescriptlang.org/)
- [Clerk](https://clerk.com/) — authentication
- [Supabase](https://supabase.com/) — database and row-level security
- [Vapi](https://vapi.ai/) — AI voice conversations
- [Vercel](https://vercel.com/) — deployment
